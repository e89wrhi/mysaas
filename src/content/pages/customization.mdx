Every user and team works differently.  
That’s why **AI Note & To-Do App** is built with customization in mind, so you can shape the experience to match your workflow.

---

## Personalization

- **Themes**: Switch between light, dark, or custom color themes.  
- **Layouts**: Choose between compact, detailed, or Kanban-style task views.  
- **Shortcuts**: Define custom keyboard shortcuts to speed up note-taking and task management.  

---

## Smart AI Preferences

- **AI Summaries**: Decide how detailed you want your AI-generated summaries to be (brief, standard, or in-depth).  
- **Task Suggestions**: Enable or disable automatic task creation from notes.  
- **Tone & Style**: Configure AI responses to be formal, casual, or technical, depending on your needs.  

---

## Team & Collaboration

- **Shared Workspaces**: Organize projects with shared folders and note libraries.  
- **Custom Roles & Permissions**: Decide who can create, edit, or assign tasks.  
- **Templates**: Save and reuse templates for meeting notes, checklists, or project plans.  

---

## Integrations

- **Calendar Sync**: Connect with Google Calendar, Outlook, or iCal to link tasks with events.  
- **Messaging Apps**: Send notes and reminders directly to Slack or Microsoft Teams.  
- **Project Management Tools**: Integrate with Trello, Jira, or Notion for seamless workflows.  

---

## Enterprise Customization

For larger organizations, we offer advanced customization options:  

- **White Labeling**: Add your company’s logo, brand colors, and domain.  
- **Dedicated AI Models**: Train custom AI models on your internal documentation.  
- **API Access**: Build custom integrations and automate workflows.  

---

## Your Workflow, Your Way

Whether you’re a solo user who just wants a clean personal setup or a large enterprise needing deep customization,  
**AI Note & To-Do App** adapts to you — not the other way around.
