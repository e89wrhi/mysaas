Every seller and brand has a unique voice and strategy.  
That’s why **Port** is built with deep customization options, allowing you to tailor the AI and the output to match your brand identity perfectly.

---

## Listing Style & Tone

- **Brand Voice**: Set your preferred tone for product descriptions—from luxury and sophisticated to friendly and conversational.
- **Content Templates**: Create and save custom listing structures for different categories (e.g., jewelry vs. electronics).
- **Keyword Prioritization**: Tell the AI which keywords or features to emphasize for specific types of products.

---

## Smart AI Preferences

- **AI Model Selection**: Choose between different AI models optimized for different marketplaces (Amazon, Etsy, etc.).
- **Automatic Background Removal**: Configure default background styles—solid white, transparent, or lifestyle mockups.
- **Custom Image Processing**: Define how you want your product photos to be enhanced (brightness, contrast, cropping).

---

## Marketplace Integration

- **Platform-Specific Rules**: Set custom rules for each connected marketplace (e.g., different pricing strategies for eBay vs. Shopify).
- **Auto-Publishing**: Enable automatic publishing for approved listings or keep them in draft mode for final review.
- **Category Mapping**: Customize how your products are categorized across different platforms automatically.

---

## Team & Enterprise Customization

- **Collaborative Workspaces**: Organize projects by brand or department with shared image libraries.
- **Custom Roles**: Define who can upload images, who can edit listings, and who can publish to marketplaces.
- **API & Webhooks**: Building custom tools? Use the Port API to push images and receive listing data programmatically.

---

## Enterprise White Labeling

For large agencies and franchises, we offer advanced white-labelling:

- **Custom Branding**: Use your own logo, brand colors, and custom domain for the Port dashboard.
- **Dedicated AI Training**: Train the AI on your historical top-performing listings to replicate your exact style.
- **Bulk Automation**: Custom workflows for high-volume listing generation and sync.

---

## Your Sales, Your Style

Whether you’re a solo creator with a specific aesthetic or a large agency managing dozens of brands,  
**Port** adapts to your unique requirements—ensuring every listing looks and feels exactly how you intended.
