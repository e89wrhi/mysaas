## Consent

By creating an account or using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.  
If you do not agree, you should stop using the app immediately.

---

## Information We Collect

When you use **AI Note & To-Do App**, we may collect:

- **Account Information**: such as your name, email address, and authentication details when you register or sign in.  
- **Notes & Tasks Data**: the content you create (notes, to-dos, attachments) is securely stored so you can access and manage them.  
- **Usage Data**: information about how you use the app, including device type, browser, operating system, and interaction patterns (e.g., clicks, session length).  
- **AI Interactions**: queries, tasks, or commands given to the AI for processing. These may be temporarily stored for the purpose of improving results and ensuring quality responses.  
- **Payment Information** (if applicable): handled securely via third-party providers (e.g., Stripe). We do not store your payment card details on our servers.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of the app.  
- Generate intelligent task suggestions and summaries using AI.  
- Sync your notes and tasks across devices.  
- Send you service-related notifications, such as updates or reminders.  
- Ensure security, prevent fraud, and monitor system performance.  
- Improve our AI models and personalize your experience (with anonymized and aggregated data).  
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is encrypted both **in transit** and **at rest**.  
- Notes and tasks are stored in secure servers with restricted access.  
- We regularly audit our systems to detect and address vulnerabilities.  
- Third-party providers we use (e.g., for payments or cloud storage) comply with strict security standards.

---

## Sharing Your Information

We do **not sell or trade** your personal data.  
We may only share information in the following cases:

- **Service Providers**: with trusted third-party services (e.g., cloud hosting, payment processors) that help us run the app.  
- **Legal Compliance**: if required by law, regulation, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.  
- **Business Transfers**: in case of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred to the new entity.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access, update, or delete your personal information.  
- Export your notes and tasks in a portable format.  
- Restrict or object to certain processing activities.  
- Withdraw consent for data usage where applicable.

To exercise these rights, you can contact us at **privacy@ainoteapp.com**.

## Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary:

- Notes and tasks remain stored until you delete them.  
- Aggregated or anonymized usage data may be retained for research and product improvement.
