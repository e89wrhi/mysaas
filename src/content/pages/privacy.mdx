We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.  
This Privacy Policy explains how **Port** (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, and safeguards data when you use our application.

By using Port, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Consent

By creating an account or using Port, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.  
If you do not agree, you should stop using the app immediately.

---

## Information We Collect

When you use **Port**, we may collect:

- **Account Information**: such as your name, email address, and authentication details when you register or sign in.  
- **Product Content & Images**: the images you upload and the listing data (titles, descriptions, tags) generated or edited within the platform.
- **Usage Data**: information about how you use the app, including device type, browser, operating system, and interaction patterns (e.g., clicks, session length).  
- **Marketplace Connection Data**: access tokens or API keys provided to connect Port to your e-commerce marketplaces (e.g., Amazon, Etsy, Shopify). These are stored securely and used only to push or sync listing data as requested by you.
- **AI Interactions**: queries or commands given to the AI for processing. These may be temporarily stored for the purpose of improving results and ensuring quality responses.  
- **Payment Information**: handled securely via third-party providers (e.g., Stripe). We do not store your payment card details on our servers.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of the app.  
- Generate intelligent e-commerce listings, SEO tags, and descriptions using AI.  
- Synchronize your product listings with external marketplaces.
- Send you service-related notifications, such as updates, marketplace sync status, or reminders.  
- Ensure security, prevent fraud, and monitor system performance.  
- Improve our computer vision and natural language models (using anonymized and aggregated data).  
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

---

## Data Storage and Security

- Your data is encrypted both **in transit** and **at rest**.  
- Product data and marketplace credentials are stored in secure servers with restricted access.  
- We regularly audit our systems to detect and address vulnerabilities.  
- Third-party providers we use (e.g., for payments or cloud storage) comply with strict security standards.

---

## Sharing Your Information

We do **not sell or trade** your personal data or your unique product information.  
We may only share information in the following cases:

- **Marketplaces**: pushing listing data to Amazon, Etsy, eBay, etc., as directed by your account actions.
- **Service Providers**: with trusted third-party services (e.g., cloud hosting, AI processing partners, payment processors) that help us run the app.  
- **Legal Compliance**: if required by law, regulation, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.  
- **Business Transfers**: in case of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, user information may be transferred to the new entity.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access, update, or delete your personal information and product data.  
- Export your generated listing data in a portable format.  
- Restrict or object to certain processing activities.  
- Withdraw consent for data usage where applicable.

To exercise these rights, you can contact us at **privacy@port.ai**.

## Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary:

- Account data is kept until you request deletion.  
- Listing content and images remain stored until you delete them or close your account.  
- Aggregated or anonymized usage data may be retained for research and product improvement.
