## Version 2.0.0 – April 30, 2026

- **Refined Luxury UI/UX**: Completely overhauled the landing page and dashboard for a premium, minimalist experience.
- **Enhanced Computer Vision**: Improved AI accuracy for detecting product categories and materials from photos.
- **Bulk Upload 2.0**: Added support for uploading up to 500 images simultaneously with background processing.
- **New Marketplace Integrations**: Added support for TikTok Shop and Temu.
- **Performance Improvements**: Optimized image processing speeds by 40%.

---

## Version 1.8.0 – March 15, 2026

- **AI Copywriting Overhaul**: Integrated more advanced LLMs for better-sounding, high-conversion product descriptions.
- **Smart Tagging**: Automated SEO tag generation for Etsy and Amazon.
- **Multi-Store Management**: Connect and manage multiple Shopify or eBay stores from a single Port account.
- **Bug Fix**: Resolved an issue with image metadata stripping during upload.

## Version 1.5.0 – January 10, 2026

- **Background Removal**: Added built-in AI background removal for project photos to create studio-quality listings.
- **Price Suggestions**: Port now suggests competitive pricing based on identified products.
- **Mobile App Beta**: Released the first version of the Port mobile app for on-the-go listing.
