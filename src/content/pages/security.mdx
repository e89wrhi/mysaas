At **AI Note & To-Do App**, protecting your data is our top priority.  
We implement enterprise-grade security measures to ensure your notes, tasks, and personal information remain private and secure.

---

## Data Encryption

- **In Transit**: All data is encrypted using TLS 1.2+ during transmission between your device and our servers.  
- **At Rest**: Notes, tasks, and files are encrypted on our servers with AES-256.  

---

## Authentication

- **Secure Login**: Passwords are hashed and salted using industry-standard algorithms.  
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Optional MFA adds an extra layer of protection to your account.  
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: Available for enterprise customers.  

---

## Infrastructure

- Hosted on trusted, secure cloud infrastructure providers (e.g., AWS, GCP, Azure).  
- Regular security patches and updates are applied to servers and dependencies.  
- Firewalls, intrusion detection, and monitoring systems are in place to prevent unauthorized access.  

---

## AI Processing

- AI requests are processed securely.  
- Sensitive data is never used to train external AI models without your consent.  
- Enterprise customers may request dedicated or private AI model instances.  

---

## Access Controls

- Role-based access controls ensure only authorized team members can access production systems.  
- All administrative actions are logged and audited.  
- Least-privilege principles are applied across internal operations.  

---

## Compliance

We are committed to meeting industry standards and regulations:  

- GDPR and CCPA compliance for data protection.  
- SOC 2 and ISO 27001-aligned practices.  
- Data residency options for enterprise customers (on request).  

---

## Incident Response

- 24/7 monitoring for unusual activity or breaches.  
- A formal incident response plan is in place to investigate and mitigate issues quickly.  
- Affected users will be notified promptly in the event of a data breach.  

---

## Responsible Disclosure

We welcome security researchers to report vulnerabilities responsibly.

---

## Commitment to You

Your trust is essential to us. We continually improve our systems, audit our practices, and work with external experts to ensure **AI Note & To-Do App** remains safe and secure.
