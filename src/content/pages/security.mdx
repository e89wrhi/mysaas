At **Port**, protecting your data and your business assets is our top priority.  
We implement enterprise-grade security measures to ensure your product images, listing data, and marketplace credentials remain private and secure.

---

## Data Encryption

- **In Transit**: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3.
- **At Rest**: Product data, marketplace tokens, and images are encrypted on our servers using AES-256 encryption.

---

## E-commerce Platform Security

- **Secure Marketplace Sync**: We use OAuth 2.0 and official marketplace APIs (Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, etc.) to securely store and use access tokens. We never ask for or store your marketplace passwords.
- **Credential Protection**: API keys and secrets are stored in hardware-backed security modules (HSM) with restricted access.

---

## Authentication

- **Secure Login**: Passwords and session tokens are managed using high-security hashing and salting algorithms.
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: We support and recommend MFA for all accounts to provide an extra layer of protection.
- **Enterprise SSO**: Support for SAML and OpenID Connect is available for organizational security management.

---

## Infrastructure & Monitoring

- **Tier-1 Cloud Providers**: Port is hosted on globally recognized, secure cloud infrastructure with multi-zone redundancy.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: 24/7 monitoring for unauthorized access attempts, performance anomalies, and potential security threats.
- **Regular Audits**: We perform internal security reviews and third-party penetration testing to identify and resolve vulnerabilities.

---

## AI Privacy

- **Proprietary Processing**: AI listing generation is performed in isolated environments.
- **Data Usage**: Your specific product images and listing descriptions are never used to train global AI models without your explicit, opt-in consent.
- **Enterprise Isolation**: Enterprise customers can request dedicated AI instances for complete data isolation.

---

## Compliance

We are committed to meeting global data protection standards:

- **GDPR & CCPA**: Fully compliant with European and Californian data privacy regulations.
- **SOC 2 Type II**: Currently undergoing certification to validate our security, availability, and confidentiality controls.
- **PCI-DSS**: All payment processing is handled by PCI-compliant partners (Stripe).

Reliability is the foundation of Port. We are committed to evolving our security posture as threats change, ensuring that your e-commerce growth is built on a safe and trusted platform.
